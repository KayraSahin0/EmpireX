# EmpireX: Rise of the Holding

> A premium mobile business simulation game where players build a company, establish a holding, dominate global markets, and become the world's most valuable corporation.

---

## About

EmpireX is a single-player business simulation and tycoon game developed with **Unity 6 LTS**.

Players begin as a small entrepreneur and gradually build a global business empire by creating companies, managing employees, investing in research, expanding into new cities and countries, acquiring competitors, and forming a multinational holding.

The project is designed around scalability, maintainability and long-term development.
